Featured Challenge: Create a Simple Weekly Budget

Live Scenario
flag

Objective: Learn to prioritize essential expenses, plan savings, and make informed spending decisions when working with a limited amount of money.

account_balance_wallet

The Everyday Scenario

You have ₦10,000 to manage for the coming week. You need to cover essential expenses, set aside some savings if possible, and leave a little room for optional activities. Your goal is to create a sensible budget without spending more than you have.

checklist Five-Step Quick Guide

  1. 1 Review the available sum: total fixed at ₦10,000.
  2. 2 Allocate sufficient funds to vital essential needs.
  3. 3 Choose an amount to set aside for savings first.
  4. 4 Decide if you have safe margins for optional spending.
  5. 5 Ensure total spend does not breach the ₦10,000 threshold.
info There is more than one reasonable budget. Focus on explaining your priorities and keeping spending within your limits.
